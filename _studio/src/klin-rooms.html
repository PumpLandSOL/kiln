how it works
Four rooms. One oven.
01

Sealed models

Chat through Chutes and Bittensor models. Prompts are sealed before they leave the browser. Real streaming answers, live per-model prices.

02

Lent GPUs

Plug your browser in as a lender over WebSocket. Askers pay per token in credits. The lender keeps 70%, paid on every answer.

03

Private mode

A model runs entirely in your browser over WebGPU. No server, no log, no network. The oven door stays shut.

04

Sealed messages

Peer-to-peer encrypted with X25519 and AES-GCM, padded, relayed through a server that only ever sees opaque bytes. Offline mailbox included.
